06 — Voice & tone

Direct

Name the action and its result. Do not make people decode clever labels.

DO“Begin capture”
DON'T“Unleash your voice”

Private

Explain where data goes in concrete terms. Privacy is a product fact, not a mood.

DO“Audio stays on this device.”
DON'T“Your privacy is our highest priority.”

Fast

Use short sentences and active verbs. Never confuse speed with pressure.

DO“Transcript ready. Add it to a project.”
DON'T“Amazing! Your lightning-fast transcript is complete!”
